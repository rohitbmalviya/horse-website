Our Selection Criteria

How We Select Our Athletes

Less than 3% of sales yearlings meet our physical, biomechanical, and veterinary standards.

Biomechanical Conformation

Balance, depth of girth, shoulder angle, and stride length measured using digital motion capture before auction bidding.

Cardio & Genetic Index

Pedigree dosage profiling matched with proven metropolitan sire nick ratings for peak speed-to-stamina distribution.

Veterinary Diagnostics

Full 5-stage pre-purchase vetting, 36-panel digital X-rays, and endoscopic evaluation conducted by independent equine surgeons.

Group-Winning Trainers

Each horse is aligned with trainers specifically proven with that bloodline, ensuring tailored race programming from day one.
